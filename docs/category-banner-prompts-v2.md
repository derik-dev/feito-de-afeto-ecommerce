# Banners panorâmicos de categorias

Gerados em 11/11/2026 pela ferramenta integrada ImageGen. Arquivos finais em `public/images/categorias/*-panoramico.webp`. Artes em proporção 3:1 cobrindo toda a área dos banners; fontes e textos acessíveis em HTML. No celular, usam-se as imagens 3:2 da primeira geração para preservar o enquadramento.

Tipografia: Manrope para Agendamento, Cormorant Garamond para Religiosas e Allura para Jardim Encantado.

## agendamento

Image aspect ratio exactly 3:1, 2400 by 800 if possible. The artwork must span the entire rectangle edge to edge as ONE continuous photographic scene, not a panel beside a photo. Scene: dramatic sophisticated burgundy and dusty rose stationery studio with soft folds of rose linen continuing across the entire left side, subtle warm shadows, tasteful small brass accents. Rightmost 45%: an open beautifully crafted spiral-bound hourly appointment diary and a closed burgundy planner with tiny gold botanical ornament, slim gold pen, a few dried flowers, refined professional feminine energy. Left 45% is quiet rose linen and warm soft light, a real textured photographed setting for a later dark burgundy HTML headline. Tiny decorative botanical sprigs at far lower left edge, no busy details under text. Stationery sits fully within right half, no objects cropped at top/bottom. Premium editorial e-commerce campaign photograph, natural warm daylight, dusty blush, burgundy and gold. No typography, text, letters, logos, borders, UI, dividers, blank white panel or watermark. This is a wide horizontal banner artwork, not a square product photograph.
